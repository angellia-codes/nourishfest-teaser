# Shot list (approved in the design spec, 13 Nov 2026)

## 5. Beat sheet

### Scene 1 — PRESS PLAY (bars 1–3, 0.00–5.63 s). Music: filtered intro, room hum, no full drums
| Cue | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1.1 | Dark room. CRT TV on a low table, lit only by off-screen pink and green neon. Slow push-in. The remote slides into the foreground, out of focus |
| 1.3 | **POWER** button presses, LED blinks. CRT power-on: dot → line → grey-blue static raster. No white flash |
| 2.1 | Tracking settles. On the screen: `theme1` in chrome and neon, `theme2` below, `osdChannel` top left |
| 3.1 | **PLAY** button presses. `osdPlay` replaces `osdChannel` |
| 3.3–4.1 | Camera rushes into the screen through scanlines and the RGB phosphor grid. **Glitch 1** on 4.1 as the cut |

### Scene 2 — BACK TO THE 90s (bars 4–6, 5.63–11.25 s). Full break from 4.1
One shot per 2 beats, linked by whip pans and rolls (motion blur on the pans):

| Cue | Shot |
|---|---|
| 4.1 | Cassette spinning toward camera, reels turning, `tapeLabel` readable |
| 4.3 | Boombox; speaker cones pump on each kick, EQ LEDs |
| 5.1 | **Glitch 2** as the cut. Vinyl: camera skims the grooves, tonearm drops |
| 5.3 | Headphones orbit with a coiled cable |
| 6.1 | Abstract 90s music-video set: checker floor, tumbling squiggles, triangles, zig-zags in brand colours |
| 6.3 | Pull back: every object orbits in one shot |

### Scene 3 — COUNTDOWN (bars 7–9, 11.25–16.88 s). Drums out; snare roll and riser build
| Cue | What happens |
|---|---|
| 7.1 / 7.2 / 7.3 | **ARE · YOU · READY?** one word per beat, chrome 3D type flying out of a black void; holds to 8.1 |
| 8.1 / 8.2 / 8.3 | **3 · 2 · 1**, one per beat. Each slams with an impact, camera shake (§7) and a neon pulse on the numeral only |
| 9.1–9.4 | **YOU ARE INVITED TO** holds a full bar. Silence from 9.4.5 to the drop |

### Scene 4 — REVEAL (bars 10–13, 16.88–24.38 s)
| Cue | What happens |
|---|---|
| **10.1** | **The drop.** NOURISHFEST slams in: massive extruded gold with a chrome bevel, pink and green neon tube outlines. A shockwave ripples the floor. Vinyl, cassettes and the boombox orbit the title; slow camera arc |
| 10.3 | **2026** stamps in |
| 12.1 | Title rises; flat info panel builds: `infoDate` |
| 12.3 | `infoGates` |
| 13.1 | `infoVenue`; panel holds still to 14.1 |

### Scene 5 — FINAL CALL (bars 14–16, 24.38–30.00 s)
| Cue | What happens |
|---|---|
| 14.1 | A 3D VHS tape sleeve turns to face camera as the invitation; its cover reads `saveDate` |
| 14.3 | `waLine` with a generic green chat-bubble icon (not the WhatsApp logo); notification ping |
| 15.1–16.3 | **End card, fully still for 2.81 s:** wordmark NOURISHFEST 2026, `themeLine`, `endInfo`, `tagline1`, `tagline2` |
| 16.3–16.4.5 | **CRT shutdown:** picture squeezes to a horizontal line, then a dot, which fades. This is glitch event 3 |
| 16.4.5–end | Black (≈ 0.23 s) |

### 5.1 9:16 re-frame
Same cues and timing. Each shot has a per-format camera preset (closer framing, vertical orbits). Wordmark stacks NOURISH / FEST / 2026; the info panel and end card stack vertically; all inside the 9:16 safe area.
